Useful signals.

OntoCheck extracts the domain terms required by competency questions and compares them with terms defined by an ontology. Both metrics are needed: coverage without focus and focus without coverage tell different stories.

01 Coverage signal

Recall Relevance

|Ta ∩ To| |Ta|

How much of the task-required vocabulary the ontology defines.

Higher means Broader task coverage
02 Focus signal

Precision Accuracy

How much of the ontology vocabulary is exercised by the task.
